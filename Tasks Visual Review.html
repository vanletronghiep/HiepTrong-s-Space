Tasks visual review

Part A lists every picture used by /tasks/daily and /tasks/hub with a verdict, plus a short site-wide clean-up list. Part B shows three paper sticky-note designs for the Tasks hub card. Nothing in the repository was changed.

The short version

  1. Remove celestial-scroll-thumb.svg: nothing references it now that the Daily scrollbar is going.
  2. Replace day-arc-decoration.svg (barely visible) with celestial-coordinate-rule.svg, and constellation-sprinkles.svg (reads as smudges) with meteor-rain-edge.svg or nothing.
  3. Modify nebula-clouds.svg: it renders as a flat band and is used three times. Soften its top edge, or keep it only in the sky bank. Thicken the strokes of card-celestial-corner.svg.
  4. Decide starlight-hourglass.svg never renders on the hub (the wrapper that draws it is skipped for all 13 themes). Put it beside the "Tasks hub" heading or delete it.
  5. Keep the planet, orbiting moon, moon-phase divider, thread divider, zodiac wheel, bloom corner and telescope. Site-wide, about 6.5 MB can go after your confirmation (4k Earth textures and the zodiac manifest).
7 keep3 modify2 replace1 remove

The running dev server returned errors on /tasks/daily and the hub list stayed on its loading skeleton (other sessions are editing those files), so I could not screenshot the two pages. Verdicts come from each file rendered on the lavender background, plus the opacity, size and mask values in the CSS. Judge faint assets in the live page before deleting.

Part A

Asset audit

Thumbnails sit on the lavender page colour. Several assets are drawn very faintly on purpose, so a faint thumbnail is not a defect by itself; the notes say when the on-page opacity makes it worse.

ringed-planet.svg

Keep2.8 KB

Daily sky top-right (opacity .58), compact day cards every 3rd (.35), "Tomorrow" empty state (daily-foundation.module.css:83, daily-task-card.module.css:26, day-task-list.module.css:92)

The best-looking asset in the set. Soft gradient, clear silhouette, reads at both 64px and 130px. Used in three places, which is fine because it is the signature shape.

orbiting-moon.svg

Keep2.6 KB

Empty-day illustration (day-task-list.module.css:88)

Clean and well balanced. Keep as the default empty-day picture.

moon-phase-divider.svg

Keep1.6 KB

Under the day header (day-carousel.module.css:102) and in the arc header (daily-foundation.module.css:352)

Delicate, legible even at .56 opacity. A good divider.

night-bloom-corner.svg

Keep1.6 KB

Every 3n+2 day card (.42) and the overdue card corner (.34) (daily-task-card.module.css:32, :100)

Pleasant, but at .34 to .42 opacity it is close to invisible. Keep, and consider raising the overdue one to about .5 if you want it noticed.

starlit-thread-divider.svg

Keep1.1 KB

Behind the Overdue section header (daily-task-card.module.css:83)

Thin and elegant. It is identical to the copy in sources/assets/daily-task-management, so nothing to swap.

zodiac-wheel.svg

Keep36.5 KB

DailySky.tsx:9, used twice: upper-left astrolabe and lower-right corner at .64

Looks good and gives the sky its character, but it is the heaviest file here (37 KB) and is decoded twice. Keep; if you ever trim, simplify the ring text first.

card-celestial-corner.svg

Modify1.4 KB

Day cards (.53) and the progress card (.6) (daily-task-card.module.css:19, daily-foundation.module.css:257)

The constellation-and-orbit line is so thin that at .53 opacity it looks like a stain on some screens. Increase stroke width about 1.5x, or drop the opacity mask. Alternative: swap to meteor-quill.svg from the Hub set for variety.

Candidate

nebula-clouds.svg

Modify9.2 KB

Sky bottom bank (.72), forecast card (.54), whole board background (.62) (daily-foundation.module.css:89, :250; day-carousel.module.css:80)

Rendered alone it is a flat horizontal band with a hard top edge. It is used in three places and the repeated band is the least refined thing on the page. Soften the top edge with a vertical fade, or reduce to one use (sky bank only).

constellation-sprinkles.svg

Replace16.7 KB

Board background top (day-carousel.module.css:79, at .62)

At this opacity it reads as smudges, not stars (17 KB for very little visible detail). Either remove, or replace with the thin meteor trails for a crisper sky.

Candidate

day-arc-decoration.svg

Replace2.4 KB

Arc header bottom (.9) (daily-foundation.module.css:346)

Almost nothing is visible; it is a faint blur line. It is not carrying any design weight. Replace with the coordinate rule, which has real tick marks and a sextant centre.

Candidate

celestial-scroll-thumb.svg

Not referenced anywhere in src/ or the working tree (grep, 0 hits)

Belonged to the custom Daily scrollbar. Safe to delete once the scrollbar removal lands (608 B).

Hub empty states (HubList.tsx:411, :428 via ui/EmptyState.tsx, 160x140)

Friendly and matches the lavender theme. Only appears when a filter or search has no results.

starlight-hourglass.svg

Modify1.8 KB

Intended header art for /tasks/hub, but never rendered (see note)

The hub is in FULL_PAGE_FEATURE_THEMES, so WorkspaceShell skips the FeatureTheme wrapper that draws this image. No other file mentions it. It is invisible today. Either place it beside the "Tasks hub" heading (it suits it) or delete it.

What would change in code

Only task-note.module.css and the hub branch of TaskCard.tsx (an extra decorative element: fold, tape or pin dot). Tokens already exist; no new colours.

Behaviour kept

Checkbox, menu, inspect click, keyboard, motion and the fixed two-line description slot stay as they are, so grid heights stay even.

Mockup limits

The checkbox and menu icons are approximations of the real components. Sample deadline text approximates formatHubDeadline. Fold (A) needs mask plus a drop-shadow wrapper because a mask clips box-shadow.

Variant B note

The priority badge is hidden because the tape carries the same word. Tape is decorative-looking but keeps its text, so screen readers still hear the priority.
